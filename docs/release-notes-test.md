Carriage version 4 — a night train, a night crossing, and a garden print. Test build for Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 12 or later). The Orient-Express release is this same build under its own name.

What's in it
- The clocks are gone. The new tab page is the inside of a Pullman carriage at night: mahogany panelling, brass bands and nameplate, and four arched windows in a row
- One landscape runs past behind all four windows and carries across the piers between them, so the row reads as a single carriage in motion: hills, woods, a lit cottage, telegraph poles and wires, and the lamp reflected in the glass. The nearer a thing is, the faster it goes by
- Flimsy slatted shutters hang in the first three windows, each pulled down as far as the last passenger left it, each leaning its own way and rattling with the car. The last window is left clear, and the moon rides outside it
- A second scene, the Le Havre – New York crossing at night: four brass portholes in the same mahogany wall, the deck beyond them empty (a lifebuoy on the rail, two folded chairs, a ventilator, no one about), and the sea. The ship carries the deck along with you, so only the sea moves: the swell runs aft and the whole sea rolls gently about one point, keeping the horizon a single line across the portholes. The moon and its path on the water sit in the last porthole
- A third scene, a Japanese print of a garden at dusk: four sheets of a woodblock print hung on the same wall, one garden running across them in the ukiyo-e manner — flat colour, a dark contour round everything, a Prussian-blue sky shading down to the evening glow. A pine reaching in from the top left, a lit stone lantern, a vermilion arched bridge over the pond, geese, raked gravel round the stepping stones, a far Fuji and a maple in autumn colour, with a title cartouche and seal. No one in it; only a few maple leaves come down
- A small brass switch under the nameplate, RAIL, SEA or GARDEN, chooses the scene; the choice is remembered on the Mac
- Nothing is fetched: all three scenes are drawn by the page itself. The weather plaques and their Open-Meteo requests are gone with the clocks, so the new tab page makes no outside connection at all
- The motion stops for anyone who asks their Mac to reduce motion
- Built on Helium 0.19.2.1 / Chromium 155.0.8059.39 (ungoogled-chromium), uBlock Origin included
- Chrome Web Store extensions install and update directly from Google, as in Chrome and Brave

Install
1. Open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.
2. This build is not yet signed by Apple: the first time, right-click it in Applications and choose Open.
